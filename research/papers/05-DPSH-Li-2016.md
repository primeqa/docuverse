# DPSH — Deep Pairwise-Supervised Hashing

**Authors:** Wu-Jun Li, Sheng Wang, Wang-Cheng Kang
**Venue:** IJCAI 2016
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.03855

## Verified key claim

DPSH is **the first method to perform simultaneous feature and binary-code
learning under pairwise similarity labels with deep networks.** **2-1 verified**
(one dissent on strict "first" — earlier two-stage methods like CNNH 2014 existed,
but DPSH is the first *simultaneous + pairwise + deep* method).

## Mechanism

End-to-end CNN producing continuous outputs `u_i ∈ R^c` per image. Loss:

```
L = − Σ_{(i,j) ∈ S} [ s_{ij} · Θ_{ij} − log(1 + exp(Θ_{ij})) ]
    + η · Σ_i || b_i − u_i ||²

where Θ_{ij} = (1/2) · u_iᵀ u_j  and  b_i = sgn(u_i)
```

- The first term is the negative log-likelihood of pairwise labels under a
  sigmoid of inner product.
- The second term is a quantization regularizer pushing continuous outputs toward
  ±1.

At inference: `b_i = sgn(u_i)`.

## Why it matters

- **Inaugurated the deep-pairwise-hashing line.** Successors (HashNet, Greedy
  Hash, CSQ) all build on this template.
- Drop-in: any CNN encoder can be retrofitted with the DPSH head + loss.

## Limitations

- Quadratic in pair count; doesn't scale to massive supervision.
- Quantization gap (continuous `u` vs. binary `b`) is not eliminated, only
  regularized.
